# Decision Brief

Use MPtrj as the broad non-OMat24 stress source, JARVIS DFPT as the matched L2
stress/BEC/piezoelectric source, and new paired constrained-spin VASP only for
identifiable L3 magnetoelastic stress. Preserve one generalized energy and
derive all response tensors. Do not enable the magnetoelastic loss until real
paired outputs pass convergence and grouping gates.
